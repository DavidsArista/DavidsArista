# David Arista

CS senior at UC Davis. Currently building production LLM systems as an AI/ML engineering intern at the City of Sacramento.

## What I work on

**SacGPT** is a RAG chatbot used by around 1,000 City of Sacramento employees. Built on Google ADK with Vertex AI and Elasticsearch (hybrid BM25 + ELSER). I shipped the two-hop labor-agreement agent tool that resolves a job title to its bargaining unit and then to the relevant MOU content, plus the ADK eval framework that runs our production test cases. Source is closed since it's an internal government deployment.

**[PokeMe](https://github.com/DavidsArista/pokeme)** is an iOS app I co-founded, built on the Claude API.

**OppsBot** is in progress: an AI categorizer for the national ColorStack Slack opportunities channel. I'm Outreach Director for ColorStack at UC Davis.
